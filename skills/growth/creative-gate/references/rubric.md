# Ad creative rubric

Score each criterion from 0 to 10, with one line of evidence. **Overall = the average,
capped at the lowest score plus 3**, so one bad criterion can't hide behind good ones.

| Criterion | 10 looks like | 0 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | The first glance (or first second of video) names a pain or outcome the viewer recognizes | Logo, slow intro or nothing to stop for |
| **One message** | One idea, one promise | Several features competing |
| **Readability** | Headline readable at phone size; 8 words or fewer; strong contrast; inside safe zones | Small or crowded text; text clipped by the platform's interface |
| **Proof** | Shows the real product (its actual screens, or the product itself) or a specific, true detail | Vague claims, stock imagery with no product |
| **Fit with the research** | The angle comes from a quoted customer pain or desired outcome | Generic copy that could sell any product |
| **Brand** | Colours, type and voice match `brand.md`; logo correct | Off-brand colours or fonts; distorted logo |
| **Call to action** | Clear next step that matches the offer (for example "Start your free week") | Missing, or promises something the destination doesn't deliver |
| **Craft** | No generation artifacts: text spelled right, hands, faces and devices look real, no warped UI | Garbled text, extra fingers, melted phone screens, fake-looking UI |

**Video extras** (fold into Hook and Craft): captions present and in sync; the narration
passed `/script-read-check`; lip sync holds; the last frame shows the CTA.
