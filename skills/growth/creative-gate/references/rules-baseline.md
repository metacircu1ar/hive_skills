# Baseline ad rules

*Apply to every product. The product's own `ad-rules.md` adds category rules (health,
finance, kids and so on). Each rule is pass, fail or not applicable. Ad-platform
policies change: before a product's first paid campaign, read the current policies of
the platforms it will run on and update its `ad-rules.md`.*

## Claims

- **true-claims-only:** every feature, number or result shown is in the product
  profile's allowed claims. Nothing from a roadmap.
- **never-claims:** nothing from the product profile's "never claim" list appears, in
  words or in pictures.
- **offer-terms:** "free", discounts and prices match the destination exactly (for
  example, "free" only as a free trial when the product is a subscription).
- **real-reviews-only:** no invented testimonials, ratings, user counts or awards.

## People and likeness

- **rights-cleared:** every real person shown has agreed, or the image is licensed for
  advertising.
- **no-remixed-real-people:** no AI person generated from a real person's photo without
  that person's consent.
- **ai-label:** AI-generated people or voices carry the platform's AI label where the
  platform requires it, and aren't presented as real customers.
- **no-personal-attributes:** copy doesn't assert or imply the viewer's health,
  finances, appearance or other personal attributes ("Struggling with debt?"). Meta
  enforces this strictly.

## Platforms and marks

- **platform-marks:** store badges (App Store, Google Play) are the official artwork,
  unaltered; no platform logos or product names used in ways their guidelines don't
  allow.
- **destination-match:** screens and features shown match the current product at the
  destination URL.

## Brand

- **brand-name:** the product's name is written as in its product profile.
- **legible-cta:** the call to action is readable at phone size.
