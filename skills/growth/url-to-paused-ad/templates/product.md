- **Competitors (`competitor`):** names.
- **Competitor policy:** `target` (competitor searches are a deliberate strategy: keep
  them, ideally in their own campaign, and negate one only on bad numbers) or `avoid`
  (treat them like `adjacent`).
- **Related, but not this product (`adjacent`):** the jobs people confuse with yours.
- **Protected terms (never negated):** your brand names and spellings, comma-separated.
